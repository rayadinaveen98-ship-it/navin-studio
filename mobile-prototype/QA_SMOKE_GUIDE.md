# Functional acceptance scenarios

1. Open HTML on desktop. Verify mock device appears and no horizontal overflow.
2. Use five bottom tabs (Home / Explore / Releases / Journal / Studio).
3. Tap **Apps** category on Home and verify Explore shows app projects.
4. In Explore, tap **Saved**; if empty, it displays a friendly empty state.
5. Open **Backlot**, tap heart, dismiss sheet, revisit Saved and check Backlot appears.
6. Open **Frame One**, verify details, public repository link and truthful no-approved-release status.
7. Open search; type `CineRelay`; verify exactly one result. Select it to open its detail sheet.
8. Press Escape or tap outside the sheet to dismiss.
9. Open Releases and confirm no version, APK size, hash, verification badge or download CTA is invented.
10. Open Journal; the foundation milestone is factual and later roadmap steps are described as future plans.
11. Toggle reduced-motion in Studio and verify the control changes.
12. Repeat on desktop and phone width; check navigation remains available and content scrolls within the device.

The code is **a prototype**, not an installable APK. All release states are previews rather than real-time verification.
